Show the computation and give the result as a percentage." Canonical rubric: daily joint failure is 0.10 x 0.20 = 0.02; no joint failure in five days is 0.98^5; requested probability is 1 - 0.98^5 = 0.0960792032, approximately 9.61 percent. - [ ] Physics question, exact text: "A 2.0 kg block starts from rest, descends a frictionless track through a vertical height of 5.0 m, and then travels 10.0 m across a horizontal surface with kinetic-friction coefficient 0.20. Canonical rubric: initial potential energy is 98 J; friction removes 39.2 J; final kinetic energy is 58.8 J, so the block reaches the end and v = sqrt(58.8) = approximately 7.67 m/s. Equivalent correct reasoning and rounding receive full credit.

A materially accurate alternative synthesis may receive full credit.
- [ ] Chemistry question, exact text: "A 1.00 L buffer contains 0.20 mol acetic acid and 0.10 mol acetate. The pKa of acetic acid is 4.76. After 0.050 mol HCl is added with negligible volume change, estimate the pH. Show the strong-acid stoichiometry before applying the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation." Canonical rubric: H+ consumes acetate, leaving 0.050 mol acetate and producing 0.250 mol acetic acid; pH = 4.76 + log10(0.050/0.250) = approximately 4.06. Equivalent correct reasoning and rounding receive full credit.
- [ ] Economics question, exact text: "A competitive market has demand Qd = 120 - 2Pb and supply Qs = 20 + 3Ps, where Pb is the price buyers pay and Ps is the price sellers receive. A tax of 10 dollars per unit is collected from sellers, so Pb = Ps + 10.
